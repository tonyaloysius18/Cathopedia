Cathopedia: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Cathopedia is a free Catholic app published by YNOT Labs, a micro-enterprise registered in France (SIRET 108 766 288 00014; 5 Rue Arnaud Baric, 31300 Toulouse, France). It has no purchases, no subscriptions and no advertising. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Cathopedia does not ask you to create an account, does not track you, and contains no advertising or analytics. Almost everything you do in the app stays on your device. The only time information leaves your device is when you choose to send us feedback.

Information that stays on your device

The app stores your settings (language, theme, text size), your bookmarks, recently viewed pages, search history, prayer progress and Rosary journal on your phone or tablet. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use the Feedback screen, we receive the category and message you write, any photos you attach, and, if you leave the option switched on, the app version and the type of device system (Android or iOS). We do not ask for your name or email address; if you write them in your message, we receive them. Feedback is delivered to us through Google Apps Script, a service of Google LLC, and stored in our Google account. We use it only to answer you and to improve the app, and keep it for up to two years.

Notifications

Feast-day reminders are scheduled on your device. No information is sent to us or to anyone else to deliver them. You can turn them off in the app's settings or your device's settings.

Read-aloud

Prayers are read aloud by your device's own text-to-speech engine.

Links to other websites

Some buttons open other websites in your browser, such as the full daily readings on bible.usccb.org or a church's location in a maps app. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Cathopedia is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you may ask to see, correct or delete any feedback you have sent us, and you may complain to the French data protection authority (CNIL, www.cnil.fr). Because we keep no other personal data, there is nothing else to access or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
