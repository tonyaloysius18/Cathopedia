# Biblical Characters — image generation brief

91 figures for the **Biblical Characters** Explore hub, one image per figure.

House style: **classical academic sacred oil painting** — fine brushwork, warm
golden light, rich earthy palette, reverent and still. The same treatment used
across the app (saints, the Holy Mass ministers, the sacraments carousel).

## Rules for every image

- **Transparent background is required** — export a PNG with a real alpha
  channel. No landscape, architecture, ground, sky, or frame: just the figure,
  isolated on transparency, so it drops straight onto the app's own surface.
- **One subject per image.** A single figure (or a figure with the infant
  Jesus / a small attribute), never a row, grid, or scene with a crowd.
- **Half-to-three-quarter length portrait**, the figure roughly centred with
  breathing room on all sides, facing the viewer or in gentle three-quarter
  view. Keep the whole subject inside the frame.
- Period-appropriate ancient Near-Eastern or first-century dress; each figure
  carries the **identifying attribute** named below so it reads instantly.
- Warm, dignified faces. **No text, no labels, no arrows, no haloed names, no
  watermark, no signature.**
- Square **1:1, 1024×1024**.

Filenames match the content asset paths: save each as
`char_<slug>.png` in `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/`
(the app resolves `hub/biblical/char_<slug>.png` by basename). Optimise to
512px like the symbols set: `sips -Z 512 in.png --out out.png` (no format flag,
keeps alpha).
