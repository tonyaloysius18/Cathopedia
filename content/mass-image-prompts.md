# The Holy Mass — image generation brief

Eight images for a new "The Holy Mass" hub:
- **6 minister figures** for "The Entrance Procession" stepper (one per step).
- **2 sacred-object images** for "The Monstrance" and "The Thurible" articles.

House style: classical academic sacred oil painting with fine detail, warm
golden light, rich earthy palette, reverent and still. **No text, no labels,
no arrows, no watermark, no signature.**

## Rules for every image

- **Transparent background is required** — export a PNG with a real alpha
  channel. No church interior, no floor, no pews, no frame: just the figure or
  object, isolated on transparency, so it drops onto the app's own surface.
- **One subject per image.** Never a row, grid, or composite infographic.
- Square **1:1, 1024×1024**.

---

## The 6 procession figures (stepper)

All six are **altar servers shown full-length, walking in gentle profile
(facing right), vested in a black cassock under a white surplice**, reverent
and unhurried — matching each other as a set. Keep the whole figure inside the
frame with a little margin.

- **mass_thurifer** — a server carrying a golden **thurible (censer)** on its
  chains in front of him, a wisp of incense smoke rising; a small incense boat
  in his other hand.
- **mass_crossbearer** — a server holding a tall **processional crucifix** on a
  staff upright before him.
- **mass_candlebearer** — a server carrying a tall **lit processional candle**
  in a holder, both hands steadying it.
- **mass_bellringer** — a server raising a small **hand bell**, caught mid-ring.
- **mass_bookbearer** — a server carrying a closed, ornate **Gospel book**
  held reverently against his chest.
- **mass_priest** — the **priest** (not a server) in a **green chasuble** over
  an alb, hands joined in prayer, walking in the same gentle right-facing
  profile, dignified as the one who presides.

## The 2 sacred objects (articles)

Rendered as a **single, detailed gilded object, centered and upright**, warm
gold with fine engraving, a soft sheen — jewel-like, reverent. Transparent
background, no exploded parts, no labels.

- **mass_monstrance** — a tall golden **monstrance**: a round central window
  (ostensorium) ringed by a radiant sunburst of rays, topped by a small cross,
  on a slender shaft with a decorated node and a broad footed base. A round
  white host visible in the centre.
- **mass_thurible** — a golden **thurible (censer)**: a rounded bowl and a
  pierced domed lid topped with a small cross, hanging from three fine chains
  gathered to a ring at the top, a faint curl of incense smoke rising.
