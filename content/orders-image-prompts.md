# Religious Orders — image prompts: FOUNDER + EMBLEM

For each order: a **founder portrait** and its **emblem**, where both exist (some have founder only, a couple emblem only). 89 images total (49 founders + 40 emblems).

- **Founders** — classical academic sacred oil painting, half-length, warm golden light, transparent background, no scenery, the founder in the order's habit. Save as `order_<slug>_founder.png`.
- **Emblems** — gilded heraldic device, gold on deep midnight, transparent background, centred, no scene (the traditional emblem, not a modern trademarked logo). Save as `order_<slug>_emblem.png`.

All: 512px transparent PNG in `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/` (resolves by basename; content paths `hub/orders/order_<slug>_founder.png` and `..._emblem.png`).

**Negative (founders):** `text, labels, watermark, signature, frame, modern clothing, cartoon, low detail, extra limbs, distorted hands`
**Negative (emblems):** `photograph, realistic face, human figure, modern corporate logo, trademark, text paragraph, watermark, signature, frame, busy background`
