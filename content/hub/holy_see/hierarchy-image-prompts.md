# Church Hierarchy & Cardinals — image generation brief

Seven images for the two new Holy See sections:
- **6 rank figures** for "The Hierarchy of the Church" stepper (one per rank).
- **1 hero image** for "The College of Cardinals" article.

Same house style as the Sacraments set: classical academic sacred oil painting,
warm golden light, rich earthy palette, fine brushwork, reverent and still, no
text, no watermark, no signature.

## Rules for every image

- **Transparent background is required** — export a PNG with a real alpha
  channel. No environment, no room, no sky, no frame: just the figure(s),
  isolated on transparency, so it drops onto the app's own green/gold surface.
- **The 6 rank figures** are **head-and-shoulders busts** (they render small, so
  the face and the distinctive vesture must read at a glance). Keep the figure
  centered with a little headroom. Square **1:1, 1024×1024**.
- **The cardinals hero** is a **half-length figure**, square **1:1, 1024×1024**.
- Vest each rank correctly and distinctly (see each prompt). Faces reverent and
  dignified.
- **No text, badges, labels or halos.** One figure group per image; never a grid.

---

## The 6 rank figures (hierarchy stepper)

- **hierarchy_laity** — Bust of the lay faithful: an ordinary devout man and
  woman side by side in simple modern-timeless clothing (no vestments), hands
  joined in prayer, gentle warm light. They represent the whole People of God.
- **hierarchy_deacon** — Bust of a deacon vested in a dalmatic with the stole
  worn across the left shoulder and fastened at the right side (the diaconal
  stole), hands folded, calm and youthful.
- **hierarchy_priest** — Bust of a priest in a black cassock and white surplice
  with a stole around the neck (or a simple chasuble), a small pectoral crucifix,
  serene expression.
- **hierarchy_bishop** — Bust of a bishop wearing a mitre, with a pectoral cross
  on his chest and a zucchetto visible beneath the mitre, dignified and paternal.
- **hierarchy_cardinal** — Bust of a cardinal in a scarlet cassock and scarlet
  biretta (three-ridged square cap), with a pectoral cross, solemn.
- **hierarchy_pope** — Bust of the pope in a white cassock and white zucchetto,
  a pectoral cross on his chest, serene and fatherly. Generic, not a portrait of
  any specific living or recent pope.

## The cardinals hero (College of Cardinals article)

- **cardinals_hero** — Half-length figure of a cardinal in full scarlet choir
  dress — scarlet cassock, white lace rochet, scarlet mozzetta over the
  shoulders, and scarlet biretta — a gold pectoral cross on his chest, hands
  gently clasped, a warm dignified light. Isolated on transparency.
