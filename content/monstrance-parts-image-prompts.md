# Monstrance Parts — image generation brief

Eleven images, one for each part of the monstrance, for the part cards on the
Monstrance screen. They sit next to the full monstrance image already in the
app (`mass_monstrance.png`), so they must match its look: a **detailed gilded
gold object, illustrated in the same warm engraved style**, jewel-like and
reverent.

## Rules for every image

- **Transparent background is required** — export a PNG with a real alpha
  channel. Just the single part, isolated on transparency, nothing behind it.
- **One part per image**, centred, upright, in the **same orientation** it has
  on a standing monstrance (as in the reference infographic).
- Match the gold tone and engraving of the existing full monstrance so the set
  looks like one object taken apart.
- No text, no labels, no arrows, no watermark. Square **1:1, 1024×1024**.

---

## The 11 parts

- **monstrance_cross** — the small ornate **gold cross** finial from the very
  top of the monstrance, standing upright.
- **monstrance_sunburst** — the **golden sunburst**: a full ring of radiating
  gold rays of glory (no central window frame, just the rays fanning out).
- **monstrance_ostensorium** — the **central circular gold frame** (the window
  surround), an ornate decorated gold ring with an empty round opening in the
  middle.
- **monstrance_glass_front** — a single upright **oval glass door** with a thin
  ornate gold rim and clear glass (the front cover of the luna housing).
- **monstrance_lunula** — a small **gold crescent-moon clip** (the lunula) that
  holds the host, points turned upward.
- **monstrance_host** — a plain **round white host**, softly luminous, with a
  faint IHS monogram at its centre.
- **monstrance_glass_back** — a single upright **oval glass door** with a gold
  rim, matching the front (the rear glass cover).
- **monstrance_back_cover** — an upright **oval solid gold cover** with an
  engraved surface and a small cross at the top (the back plate).
- **monstrance_node** — the **gold knop** on the stem, a rounded bulb worked
  with wheat-and-vine decoration.
- **monstrance_shaft** — the slender **gold shaft / stem** of the monstrance,
  standing vertically, with small decorative rings.
- **monstrance_base** — the broad, ornate **gold footed base** of the
  monstrance, seen from the front.

---

## Deliverable

11 **transparent PNG** files, square 1:1 (1024×1024), named exactly:
`monstrance_cross.png`, `monstrance_sunburst.png`, `monstrance_ostensorium.png`,
`monstrance_glass_front.png`, `monstrance_lunula.png`, `monstrance_host.png`,
`monstrance_glass_back.png`, `monstrance_back_cover.png`, `monstrance_node.png`,
`monstrance_shaft.png`, `monstrance_base.png`. Hand them back as a batch — they
drop straight onto the part cards.
