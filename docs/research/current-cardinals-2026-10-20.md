# Current cardinals (as of 20 October 2026)

**240 living cardinals: 116 electors** (under 80, able to vote in a conclave) and **124 non-electors**.
Pope Leo XIV has not yet created any cardinals; the most recent consistory creating cardinals was Pope Francis's on 7 December 2024.

By order: Cardinal Bishops 12, Cardinal Priests 196, Cardinal Deacons 32.
Created by: Benedict XVI 58, John Paul II 38, Francis 144.

Elector status is by age on 20 Oct 2026; Cardinal Becciu (78) renounced the rights of a cardinal in 2020 and is not counted as an elector.
Source: Wikipedia, *List of current cardinals* (revision of Oct 2026), each row cross-referenced to its Holy See Press Office biography. Machine-readable copy: `current-cardinals-2026-10-20.csv`.
