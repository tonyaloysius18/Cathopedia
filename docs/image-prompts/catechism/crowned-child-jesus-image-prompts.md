## What these are

Each image is a **statue**, not a living child. Paint it as a carved,
polychrome devotional figure, dressed in real fabric robes the way these
images are dressed in their shrines. The face should read as painted wood or
wax: serene, slightly stylised, with the stillness of a statue. It should
never look like a photograph of a baby or a toddler in costume.

The four must be **told apart at a glance**. The source infographic drew all
four as near-identical standing figures in red and gold, which is wrong for
Aracoeli (a swaddled newborn, not a standing child) and misses what makes
the others distinct. Follow the per-image notes.

## Rules for all four

- **Transparent background is required**: a PNG with a real alpha channel.
  The statue stands alone (with its own small base or pedestal where noted),
  with no altar, niche, church interior, flowers or candles.
- Square **1:1, 1024×1024**. The figure is centred, full-length, and fills
  about three-quarters of the frame height, with the crown fully in frame.
- Classical academic sacred oil-painting finish, the same as the stigmata
  saints: warm golden light from the upper left, fine detail in the
  embroidery and goldwork, reverent and still.
- **No text, no labels, no watermark, no signature, no captions.**
- After generating, check the alpha channel. Earlier cut-outs arrived with
  the transparency checkerboard baked into the edges.

**Negative prompt (all four):** `text, letters, words, labels, watermark,
signature, real baby, photograph, living child, toddler, cartoon, chibi,
anime, plastic doll, background, church interior, altar, candles, flowers,
people, hands holding it, modern clothing, low detail`
