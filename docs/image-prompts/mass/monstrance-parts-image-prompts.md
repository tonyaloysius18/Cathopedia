# Monstrance component image prompts

These eleven transparent component assets support the dedicated Cathopedia
Monstrance anatomy screen. The existing `mass_monstrance.png` is the visual
reference for the goldwork, jewels, engraving, lighting, and painterly finish.

## Shared direction

Use case: scientific-educational. Transparent component illustration for a
Catholic mobile app anatomy screen. Classical academic sacred oil-painting
realism, antique-gold metal, fine engraving, jewel-like detail. One isolated
component only, centered, front-facing, large and legible at small mobile size,
fully contained with generous transparent margin. Genuine transparent PNG
alpha; no field, card, pedestal, labels, arrows, numbers, watermark, signature,
glow panel, or scenery. Square 1:1, 1024×1024.

## Components

- `mass_monstrance_part_cross.png` — the complete ornate Latin cross and its
  short decorative mounting stem; no rays or lower components.
- `mass_monstrance_part_sunburst.png` — the complete alternating straight and
  flame-like rays with engraved circular frame and matching jewels; the central
  opening remains transparent.
- `mass_monstrance_part_window.png` — the circular ostensorium bezel and glass
  door frame; the inner opening remains transparent.
- `mass_monstrance_part_glass_front.png` — the ornate jeweled front glass door,
  with a visible hinge and clasp; both the background and the clear-glass center
  remain transparent.
- `mass_monstrance_part_luna.png` — a historically plausible engraved golden
  crescent host holder, empty and front-facing.
- `mass_monstrance_part_host.png` — one plain ivory-white round host with a
  subtle scored cross and no letters.
- `mass_monstrance_part_glass_back.png` — the simpler engraved rear glass door,
  with hinge, clasp and a clear transparent center.
- `mass_monstrance_part_back_cover.png` — the solid engraved gold closing plate;
  only the area outside the cover is transparent.
- `mass_monstrance_part_node.png` — the complete bulb-shaped node/knop with its
  engraved collars and matching jewel accents.
- `mass_monstrance_part_shaft.png` — the complete narrow upright shaft/stem,
  without the node or base.
- `mass_monstrance_part_base.png` — the broad footed base with lower mounting
  collar, engraved relief, jewels, and all feet.
