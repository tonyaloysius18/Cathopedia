# Cardinal Orders — portrait and heraldic-emblem prompts

These six assets support the Cathopedia "College of Cardinals" carousel.

Accuracy note: Cardinal Bishop, Cardinal Priest, and Cardinal Deacon are the three
orders of the College of Cardinals (Code of Canon Law, can. 350). They are not
three separate degrees of sacramental ordination and do not have three universal
official coats of arms. The emblems below are therefore deliberately described as
**illustrative order emblems**. They share cardinal heraldic language while using
different symbolic devices to help readers remember each order.

## Shared portrait direction

Classical academic sacred oil painting, warm dignified golden light, rich scarlet,
cream and antique-gold palette, fine brushwork, reverent and still. Square 1:1,
1024×1024, half-length figure centered with breathing room. Genuine transparent
PNG background; no room, architecture, sky, floor, frame, text, watermark,
signature or halo. The face must be generic and must not resemble a specific
living or recent cardinal.

### `cardinal_order_deacon.png`

A dignified generic cardinal of the diaconal order in full scarlet choir dress:
scarlet cassock, white lace rochet, scarlet mozzetta, scarlet three-ridged biretta,
cardinal ring and gold pectoral cross. Half-length, holding a closed Book of the
Gospels gently against his chest as an illustrative symbol of proclamation and
service. Do not dress him as a sacramentally ordained deacon; no diagonal stole or
dalmatic. Transparent background.

### `cardinal_order_priest.png`

A dignified generic cardinal of the presbyteral order in full scarlet choir dress:
scarlet cassock, white lace rochet, scarlet mozzetta, scarlet three-ridged biretta,
cardinal ring and gold pectoral cross. Half-length, hands joined in prayer, with a
small gold model of a Roman titular church resting lightly in one hand as an
illustrative reference to his Roman title. Transparent background.

### `cardinal_order_bishop.png`

A dignified generic cardinal of the episcopal order in scarlet choir dress with
white lace rochet, scarlet mozzetta, gold pectoral cross and cardinal ring. He wears
a complete mitre with the entire tip inside the canvas; a scarlet zucchetto is
visible beneath it. Half-length, one hand resting over his heart. No crozier and no
specific portrait likeness. Transparent background.

## Shared emblem direction

Illustrative Catholic heraldic badge, not a personal or universal official coat of
arms. Square 1:1, 1024×1024, transparent PNG. Centered symmetrical antique-gold
shield with a deep scarlet field, a red cardinal galero above it and stylized red
tassel cords framing both sides. Clean high-detail engraved/enamel finish, strong
silhouette at small mobile size, no name, motto, letters, numbers, watermark or
signature. No environment or frame beyond the emblem itself.

### `cardinal_emblem_deacon.png`

On the shield, place an open gold Book of the Gospels above a small crossed palm
branch and serving cloth. The book and service symbols represent proclamation and
service; they are mnemonic devices, not official arms.

### `cardinal_emblem_priest.png`

On the shield, place a gold chalice with a radiant white Host above it, accompanied
by a small basilica silhouette. These represent the Eucharistic ministry and the
Roman titular church; they are mnemonic devices, not official arms.

### `cardinal_emblem_bishop.png`

On the shield, place a gold mitre above a small stylized ring of seven hills and a
simple processional cross. These evoke episcopal ministry and the suburbicarian
sees around Rome; they are mnemonic devices, not official arms.
