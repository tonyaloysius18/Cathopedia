# Transparent Marian Rosary artwork

Created with the built-in image generator as a background-extraction edit of the original landing artwork. The second edit is the selected asset.

Asset: `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/rosary_landing_marian_cutout.png`

Use case: background-extraction. Make a professionally isolated, genuinely transparent PNG cutout of ONLY the blue-and-white Marian Rosary from the supplied photograph. Remove the fabric completely, and remove ALL background haze, ALL cast shadows and background pixels inside the loop, between beads, inside every chain link and around the cross. Outside the object must be fully transparent alpha zero; do not add a black, grey, white or checkerboard backdrop. Keep the same polished white cobalt floral porcelain beads, deep blue large beads, small detailed silver links, Marian medal, pearl and silver crucifix. Preserve realistic material and object lighting. Composition: reduce the object's size enough to provide clear 8% transparent padding on ALL sides, especially beyond the entire cross at the lower right. Keep EVERY part of the rosary entirely inside the frame. Landscape 3:2. Preserve the complete rosary, with no clipped cross, no clipped beads, no reordering. No UI, no text, no added elements, no floor, no fabric, no glow, no cloud, no shadow. Clean tight transparent cutout edge, with no broad translucent halo.

Verified RGBA output with fully transparent pixels inside the Rosary loop. The app preserves the alpha and adds its own layout breathing room.
