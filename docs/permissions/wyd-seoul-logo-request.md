# WYD Seoul 2027 logo — permission request (draft, not sent)

> **Status 2026-10-20:** the logo is now in the feed (`badge` on `wyd-seoul-2027`)
> at the publisher's request. Send this request and keep the written reply here;
> if consent is refused, delete `badge` and `badgeCredit` from the item on `main`.

The WYD Seoul 2027 Foundation grants free use of the WYD Seoul titles and
logo only to *entities of the Catholic Church*, for promotion of and spiritual
preparation for WYD. Everyone else needs the Foundation's **prior written
consent** (wydseoul.org/en/introduction/logo). YNOT Labs is not a Church
entity, so Cathopedia must ask before showing the logo. Until a written yes
arrives, the WYD card uses only the official colours with our own drawing.

**To:** commu@wydseoul.org
**Subject:** Request to use the WYD Seoul 2027 logo in Cathopedia, a free Catholic app

---

Dear WYD Seoul 2027 communications team,

My name is Tony Aloysius. I publish **Cathopedia**, a free Catholic
encyclopedia app for Android and iOS, through YNOT Labs in France. The app
has no ads, no in-app purchases and no paid features, and it will stay free.
It is available in nine languages: English, French, Spanish, Italian,
Portuguese, German, Dutch, Polish and Tamil.

The app has a "Catholic News" section on its home screen. One of its cards
presents WYD Seoul 2027: the dates (3–8 August 2027), the theme "Take courage!
I have overcome the world", a countdown, a short description, and a button
that opens wydseoul.org.

We would like your written permission to show the **official WYD Seoul 2027
main logo** on this card and on its detail page, until the end of the event.
We would:

- use the logo files from your website without changing them, following
  your brand guidelines (colours, clear space, minimum size);
- show it only next to information about WYD Seoul 2027, and always link to
  wydseoul.org;
- add the credit "Logo © WYD Seoul 2027 Foundation, used with permission";
- remove it whenever you ask.

I would be glad to send screenshots of the card before it is published. Thank
you for your work in preparing this gathering for young people from all over
the world.

With gratitude and prayers,

Tony Aloysius
Cathopedia — YNOT Labs, France
tony.aloysius18@gmail.com
