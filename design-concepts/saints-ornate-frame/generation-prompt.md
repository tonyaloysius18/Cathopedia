# Saints card frame

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool using the user's gold frame as the reference.
The generated PNG has a transparent centre and background and is bundled as
`shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/saints_feast_ornate_frame.png`.

## Final prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: a transparent decorative border overlay for a Cathopedia Catholic saint hero card on a mobile screen. Reference image: the supplied gold Catholic ornamental frame. Create a new refined version of that frame, keeping the antique gold engraved acanthus scrollwork, the small central Christian cross and two inward-facing doves, but make the left and right sides considerably thinner and more delicate. Composition: a front-facing landscape rectangular frame, approximately 1.4:1 overall width-to-height, with softly rounded inner corners to suit an app card. The centre is a completely empty transparent opening, and everything outside the gilded ornament is also truly transparent. No white fill and no checkerboard. Keep all detail crisp: warm metallic gold highlights, deeper ochre engraved shadows, tasteful ecclesiastical ornament. Narrow side rails occupy at most 2.5 percent of the width on each side; elaborate small leaves belong mainly at the four corners and along the top and bottom, leaving the middle portions of both vertical rails almost straight, thin and simple so those rails can be extended vertically. The top-centre cross and two small doves form a compact crest ABOVE the upper rail, staying within the top 15 percent of the canvas. The upper rail and its corners lie around 15 percent down the canvas; the bottom rail lies around 96 percent down. Keep the overall ornament light and slim so it will not cover a saint's portrait or text when used as an overlay. Flat orthographic view, symmetrical, no perspective. No saint, no portrait, no writing, no labels, no UI, no mockup, no drop shadow beyond the gold, no opaque backdrop.

## Integration

The generated crest, corner ornaments, centre-side details and lower border retain
their proportions. Only two straight sections of the side rails extend vertically
for longer content. The original saint portrait, text and card navigation remain in place.
